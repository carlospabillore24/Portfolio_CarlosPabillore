About

Developer, business owner and marketer, usually all in the same week.

I'm Carlos, a developer based in Cagayan de Oro. I own MugTuon Learning Hub & Cafe, a study café in Nazareth, and I write the systems it runs on: the member website, the staff payroll app, the loyalty rewards card and the tablet POS.

Because I'm also the one behind the counter, I build for the day things go wrong. That means offline-first apps, payroll logic covered by tests before it touches anyone's pay, two security audits before going live, and deploys that work on ordinary shared hosting.

Outside MugTuon I build Shopify themes for brands with NC Marketing, and I read Meta Ads data to decide what to build next.
